二、世界模型：预测世界，而非预测"车该怎么开"

[04:05 – 05:11]

You've been building generative AI world models since 2018, right? And I believe you call them Gaia's. There's a very talented team that's behind that at Wayve. But roughly speaking, world models in the industry are networks that predict the future actions of the world as opposed to predicting the actions of how a car should drive. So it's everything around the car. That is evolving. This includes all of the vehicles in the scene, all of the people, all of the background objects that might affect the driving. And it's a model that fundamentally understands physics and how the world behaves as opposed to how the ego vehicle, the AI driver should behave. And this is a super challenging problem. It's a hot topic in research today. It's one of our largest models at Wayve. Gaia, the one that you mentioned, and one that we've recently launched our fourth generation of, and that's Gaia 4.

你们从 2018 年就开始构建生成式 AI 世界模型了，对吧？而且我记得你们管它们叫 Gaia。什么是世界模型，它为什么有助于教 AI 开车？——先澄清一下，我负责的是世界模型这一侧，不是 AI Driver 那一侧，Wayve 那边有一支非常厉害的团队。但大致来说，行业里的世界模型，是去预测「世界」未来会如何行动的网络，而不是预测「车该怎么开」。所以它关注车周围的一切、那些在演化的东西：场景里所有的车辆、所有的人、所有可能影响驾驶的背景物体。它是一个本质上理解物理、理解世界如何运转的模型，而不是理解自车（也就是 AI Driver）该如何表现的模型。这是个超级难的问题，也是当下研究的热门方向。它是 Wayve 最大的模型之一。你提到的 Gaia，我们最近已经发布到第四代，也就是 GAIA-4。

So you're trying to get an exact replica of a real world situation. 所以你们是想拿到真实世界情境的一个精确复刻，把所有的微妙之处、所有盲区、所有边缘案例都梳理出来，打包进一个单一的模型里。——对，不过「精确复刻」这个词，其实可以非常微妙。我们想做的是捕捉世界里所有的细微差别—— literally 所有的细微差别：反光是怎么工作的、车辆在水坑和坑洼旁怎么表现、东京和伦敦的骑行者行为有何不同。所有这些细微差别、所有这些丰富的细节，然后生成可信的情景。有时候我们管这叫反事实（counterfactuals）。如果我们从一个真实场景出发，想生成「本来还可能发生什么」，也可以完全从零开始新颖地生成场景。但没错，我们想捕捉所有的细微差别，以尽可能真实、尽可能可信的方式，去呈现世界本可能如何演化。

四、GAIA-4：闭环仿真

[11:31 – 12:06]

Great, thank you. Let's get on to Gaia 4 then. So you've just released this new generation, this new model. This is a big milestone for Wayve, correct me if I'm wrong. What does it allow you to do that wasn't possible before? — Yeah. A couple of key things. There's many in the blog post, but I'll drill into two. One of them is closed loop, and then the other is multi-modality different sensors.

好，谢谢。那我们进入 GAIA-4。你们刚发布了新一代、这个新模型。对 Wayve 来说这是个重大里程碑，如果我没说错的话。它能让你做到哪些以前做不到的事？——对，有几个关键点。博客里写了很多，我挑两个深入讲：一个是闭环（closed loop），另一个是多模态、也就是不同的传感器。

[12:06 – 14:30]

So the first one, closed loop versus open loop. There's a nice picture that explains it a little bit better, but really in self-driving cars, autonomous vehicles, and robotics in general, you have two ways of evaluating a system offline. You have something called Open-Loop, which is replaying pre-recorded data through a new version of the software or the model and seeing what the outputs are. So that's called Open-Loop. Closed-Loop, which is the advancement with Gaia, is replaying that real world data through the autonomy stack and then taking the actions that the simulated autonomous vehicle does, the AI driver, and feeding that back into the world model and saying, well, how does the world evolve as a result of these actions? Now, that's kind of a mind blowing thing to think that you could predict, for example, so that says in simulation of the car, the AI driver decides to slow down, we should expect to see the view and the simulation to reflect slowing down. You should see the camera view from a different vantage point. And we'll get to this in a second. You should also see the radar returns from a different vantage point. If you slow down or if you speed up, you should expect to see perhaps the other actors react to you differently. And we talk about different modes of interaction. We could put Gaia and we mentioned World on Rails. There's also one that we can do with reactive agents, which is another one of the advancements mentioned. So close loop is actually absolutely critical to our offline test capability because it's how, when you and I go drive, it's how we experience the world. When we drive a certain way, when we slow down or speed up or take a left-hand turn versus a right-hand turn, we see different stuff and we feed that back into our sensors, our eyes primarily, and we drive differently. So closing the loop is a huge, huge advancement for Gaia and for these class of world models in general. It is a thing that the industry has been able to do for a while, to be clear, but with very different styles of simulation. These are more procedural simulators, rendering-based simulators, game engine-style simulators. The industry, going back to as long as I've been in it, has been able to do closed-loop simulation. This is a specific advancement about us being able to do it with a world model.

第一个，闭环对开环。有张不错的图能解释得更清楚，但说白了，在自动驾驶、自动驾驶车和广义机器人里，离线评估一个系统有两种方式。一种是开环（Open-Loop）：把预录数据重放进软件或模型的新版本，看输出是什么。这就是开环。而闭环（Closed-Loop）——这是 GAIA 带来的进步——是把真实世界数据重放进自动驾驶技术栈，然后取出模拟自动驾驶车（也就是 AI Driver）做出的动作，把它反馈回世界模型，问：这些动作导致世界如何演化？这事想起来挺震撼的——比如，在仿真里，AI Driver 决定减速，我们就应当看到仿真里的画面反映出减速：你会从一个不同的视角看到相机画面；稍后我们还会聊到，你还应当看到雷达回波也来自不同视角。你减速或加速，就应当看到其他交通参与者对你做出不同的反应。我们谈到了不同的交互模式：除了前面提到的 World on Rails，还有一种带反应式智能体（reactive agents）的模式，那也是提到的进步之一。所以闭环对我们的离线测试能力绝对关键，因为它就是你我开车时体验世界的方式——我们以一种特定方式开，减速、加速、左转还是右转，看到的东西就不一样，再把这些反馈回我们的传感器（主要是眼睛），于是我们开得不一样。所以闭环对 Gaia、对这一类世界模型整体，都是巨大的进步。不过要讲清楚：行业其实早就能做闭环了，只是用非常不同的仿真风格——更多是程序化仿真、基于渲染的仿真、游戏引擎式仿真。从我入行起，行业就能做闭环仿真了。这里具体的进步是：我们能用世界模型来做闭环。
