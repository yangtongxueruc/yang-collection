In August 2026 Waymo named Munich as its first market in continental Europe, with commercial service targeted for late 2027. The German transport ministry read that choice as validation of its own legal framework — the same framework that has produced no regular driverless passenger service in five years. This episode hands the microphone to the ministry's political side.

2026 年 8 月，Waymo 把慕尼黑定为其欧洲大陆第一个市场，商业运营目标定在 2027 年底。德国交通部把这一选择读成对自己法律框架的背书——而这同一套框架，五年里没有催生任何常态化的无人驾驶客运服务。这一期把话筒交给了部委的政治层。

[06:07 — 11:33]

Then, the industrial question. What is at stake for a car nation if value moves to software companies from elsewhere? Does it matter more that the driving software is European, or that fleets, depots and remote operations sit in Germany? And should Europe build a state-backed champion for autonomous driving, the way it built Airbus?

然后是产业之问。如果价值迁移到别处的软件公司手里，一个汽车国到底失去了什么？更重要的究竟是「驾驶软件是欧洲的」，还是「车队、场站与远程运营落在德国」？还有：欧洲该不该像当年造 Airbus 那样，用国家力量扶一个自动驾驶冠军？
