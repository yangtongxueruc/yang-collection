核心命题 · The Thesis

Robotaxis may look futuristic, but a basic economic problem still haunts them: too many miles are driven with no paying passenger. These "deadhead miles" — empty trips to distant, centralized depots for charging, cleaning and inspection — are among the biggest obstacles to profitability. Aseon Labs proposes to fix this with parking-space-sized automated "pit stop" pods distributed across the city, instead of one far-away depot.

Robotaxi 看起来很未来，却仍被一个最基础的经济问题困扰：太多里程是在没有付费乘客的情况下跑出来的。这些「空驶里程（deadhead miles）」——空车长途跑到城市边缘的集中式 depot 去充电、清洁、巡检——是盈利之路上最大的障碍之一。Aseon Labs 的提议是：用约一个停车位大小、散布全城的自动化「维修站」pod，取代那个遥远的集中式 depot。

空驶里程 · The Deadhead-Miles Problem

Today's AV depots sit on cheap land at the city's edge because downtown real estate is expensive. The result: a robotaxi may drive twenty empty miles from a warehouse on the edge just to reach you — no passenger, no revenue, just electricity. Every empty trip back is money nobody is paying for.

今天的 AV depot 大多因为市中心地价贵，而落在城市边缘的便宜地块上。结果就是：一辆 robotaxi 可能要空驶二十英里从城市边缘的仓库存活开到你面前——没有乘客、没有收入，只有耗电。每一次空车返程，都是没人买单的花费。

产品形态 · The Product

Each pod inspects the vehicle with cameras, cleans the interior and retrieves lost items with a robotic arm, and charges the car — all without the robotaxi leaving for a depot. Aseon classifies the units as temporary structures, which sidesteps slow permitting and lets it relocate a pod if a spot underperforms. Power can come from an onboard propane generator or, through tie-ups with charging companies, an existing hookup. Early units will be staffed; full autonomy is the long-term goal.

每个 pod 用摄像头巡检车辆、用机械臂清洁内饰并捡回遗留物品、为车辆充电——全程无需 robotaxi 离场去 depot。Aseon 把这些单元归类为「临时结构」，从而绕开缓慢的许可流程，并在某点位表现不佳时整体迁移。供电可来自车载丙烷发电机，也可通过与充电公司合作接现有接口。早期单元有人驻守，完全自主是长期目标。

经济账 · The Economics

"In order to reach economic parity with ride-hailing — which is where we need to get with self-driving cars — and to stop really subsidizing the cost, you need the utilization to go up. You need the robotaxi in continuous operation during the entirety of the demand curve of the day." — George Kalligeros, CEO of Aseon Labs

「要达到与网约车相当的经济性——这正是自动驾驶汽车需要抵达的位置——并停止真正补贴成本，你必须把利用率提上去。你需要 robotaxi 在一天需求曲线的全部时段里都保持连续运营。」—— George Kalligeros，Aseon Labs 联合创始人兼 CEO

智能甄别 · Smart Triage

Aseon is not trying to solve every cleaning issue blindly. Its computer vision and VLA (vision-language-action) models identify cases the pod should not handle — for example, melted chocolate on a seat — and send the vehicle to a central depot instead, after charging it. Smart triage, not blind automation.

Aseon 并不打算盲目解决所有清洁问题。它的计算机视觉与 VLA（视觉-语言-动作）模型能识别 pod 不该处理的情形——例如座椅上融化的巧克力——并在充完电后把车派往中心 depot。这是聪明的甄别，而非盲目自动化。

创始人履历 · The Founders' Track Record

Kalligeros and COO Dan Keene are not AV veterans. Kalligeros was a mechanical design engineer at Bentley Motors and Tesla, then in 2016 co-founded Pushme, a battery-swapping infrastructure startup for micromobility fleets. Pushme scaled across European markets before Tier Mobility acquired it in January 2020. The parallel they draw: SoftBank tasked the Tier team with deploying swap infrastructure across many markets in a compressed timeframe — the playbook of sprinkling lightweight, non-permanent infrastructure across city centers is exactly what Aseon applies to AV servicing.

Kalligeros 与 COO Dan Keene 都不是 AV 老兵。Kalligeros 曾在 Bentley Motors 与 Tesla 任机械设计工程师，2016 年共同创办面向微出行车队的换电基础设施公司 Pushme。Pushme 在欧洲多国扩张后，于 2020 年 1 月被 Tier Mobility 收购。他们援引的对照是：SoftBank 曾要求在极短时间内把换电基建铺到多个市场——这套「在城市中心轻量、非永久地撒点」的打法，正是 Aseon 要照搬到 AV servicing 上的。

融资与阶段 · Funding & Stage

Aseon Labs raised $10M in seed funding led by Crane Venture Partners, with Y Combinator, Expa (Garrett Camp's firm), Robin Hood Ventures and Founders Capital participating, plus angels from Anthropic, Nuro, Turo and Revolut circles. The money funds five prototype pods, grows the six-person team to ~12, and secures real estate. No operator contracts are signed yet; Kalligeros says "pretty much everyone wants to try it."

Aseon Labs 完成 1000 万美元种子轮，由 Crane Venture Partners 领投，Y Combinator、Expa（Garrett Camp 的风投）、Robin Hood Ventures、Founders Capital 参投，并有来自 Anthropic、Nuro、Turo、Revolut 圈子的天使。资金用于造 5 个原型 pod、把 6 人团队扩到约 12 人、并锁定地产。目前尚未签下任何运营商合同；Kalligeros 称「几乎人人都想试试」。

看空的逻辑 · The Skeptic's Case

The strongest argument against the model is also the most obvious: the robotaxi industry itself is still finding its footing. If major operators build proprietary servicing, or if fleet growth stalls, Aseon's addressable market shrinks. Real estate is a genuine question — distributing pods across dense cities means dozens of individual location deals, city permitting, and a fragmented footprint. Until it signs a real AV operator, the pod network exists only in prototype form.

对该模式最有力的反对意见也最直白：robotaxi 行业本身还在找 footing。如果头部运营商自建专有 servicing，或车队增长停滞，Aseon 的可寻址市场就会缩水。地产是真实难题——在密集城区铺点意味着几十个独立选址谈判、市政府许可，以及碎片化的实体足迹。在签下真正的 AV 运营商之前，这套 pod 网络只存在于原型形态。
